\section{Examples}

\textbf{Example 1}

$\{\alpha_n\}$ is an infinitesimal sequence. Let
\[\beta_n = \frac{\sum_{i = 1}^{n} \alpha_i }{n}\]
Then $\{\beta_n\}$ is also an infinitesimal sequence.
